Containers

Which container for which content. Rules V1–V11 in 2-structure.md.

Ask first: what is the reader doing with these items? The answer names the container. A card is the answer only when they pick or act on objects.

When cards are right (V4)

  • The items are objects people pick, move or act on: photos to open, outfit choices, products.
  • A card with one destination is one link; one with several keeps separate named links (never nested).
  • Separate from the ground by a tone step, not an outline or resting shadow (T2).

Source: NN/g, “Cards: UI-Component Definition”: cards suit browsing, play down ranking, and scan worse than lists.
