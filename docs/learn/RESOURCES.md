# Design principles Resources

## Knowledge

- [Phuru's rules: `~/code/design-principles`](../../../design-principles/README.md)
  The rule set this course teaches; every lesson cites its IDs (T2, C4, V7). Use for: what the rule says.
- [Taste Skill, `skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md` (Leonxlnx, MIT)](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md)
  Source of the slop tells (T2), the idea sentence and competitor test (T1), and the type/space laws, backed by a panel study of 247 real sites. Use for: why a tell is banned, with numbers.
- [Vercel, Web Interface Guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines)
  Concrete interaction and accessibility details (focus, forms, motion, URL state). Use for: stage 4 (I2–I7, ST1–ST6) and I1.
- [W3C, WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and [Understanding "Use of Color"](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html)
  The accessibility floor (P1) and why color can't carry meaning alone (C3). Use for: contrast, focus, targets.
- [Jakob Nielsen, "10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design" (NN/g)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/)
  The owner's P0 for usability: ten heuristics from 1994, refreshed through 2024, each with a deeper NN/g article. Turned into rules H1–H11, spread across the stage files (index in `5-ship.md` H11). Use for: feedback, exits, consistency, errors, help, heuristic reviews.
- [Nielsen Norman Group, "Proximity Principle in Visual Design" (Harley, 2020)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/gestalt-proximity/)
  Why space says what belongs together, and how responsive layouts break it. Use for: T4 section gaps, lesson 6.
- [Nielsen Norman Group, "Progressive Disclosure"](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/)
  Research behind showing essentials first and detail on demand. Use for: Glance / Scan / Explore (P5), expandables (V7).
- [Nielsen Norman Group, "F-Shaped Pattern of Reading on the Web"](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content/)
  How people scan pages. Use for: P5, left alignment, front-loaded headings.
- [web.dev, "Web Vitals"](https://web.dev/articles/vitals)
  Google's definitions and thresholds for LCP, INP, CLS. Use for: PF1–PF2.
- [Matthew Butterick, *Practical Typography*](https://practicaltypography.com/)
  Free, opinionated typography reference. Use for: T3, P7 (sizes, measure, line spacing, quotes).
- [Adam Wathan & Steve Schoger, *Refactoring UI*](https://www.refactoringui.com/) (paid)
  Hierarchy, spacing and color for developers who design. Use for: T4, T5, C1.
- [Rauno Freiberg, *Devouring Details*](https://devouringdetails.com/) (paid)
  Interaction craft; Skiper UI credits it. Use for: feedback motion, T8.

## Wisdom (Communities)
