# Found a design device by asking what it would look like

Lesson 3, third try (2026-10-20). His sentence's second blank was still a meaning ("the path I take to push past and learn more about me"), but his follow-up question was a real device: "do we start somewhere dark and as it goes, it becomes brighter?" A ground that steps from trailhead-dark to summit-bright, with "About me" as the view at the top, is something a designer could build. So he *can* produce a device; it shows up when he pictures the page, not when he writes the sentence.

**Evidence**: proposed dark → bright progression unprompted after the three-device nudge.

**Implications**: when he's stuck on an abstract blank, ask "what would a visitor see?" rather than "rewrite it". The stepped grounds touch color rules he hasn't learned yet (T5 proportions, C1 per-surface link tokens, C2 checks per step, C6 dark mode): a natural, motivated entry into the color lessons.
