How the rules are built

Rules live in ~/code/design-principles, one file per stage of the work (idea, structure, look, behavior, ship) plus three add-ons, each opening with a table of its IDs. Every rule has a stable ID (T2, C4, V7) so a plan or review can cite exactly which rule a change keeps or breaks. Strength words:

  • MUST: required.
  • SHOULD: the default; break it only with a stated reason.
  • CONSIDER: an idea worth trying.
  • Verify: how to check it, so “done” is provable, not a feeling.

Each project picks its own values in a DESIGN.md (this site: docs/DESIGN.md, IDs B1–B9). A project may choose colors and faces but never loosen a rule. When you correct a design call, it goes into learnings.md; once it holds up on a real project it becomes a rule and gets a CHANGELOG.md entry.

The seven beliefs, and why

BeliefWhy it mattersRules
Everyone gets inWCAG 2.2 AA is the floor. A page that fails a keyboard, a screen reader, 200% zoom or colorblind eyes shuts real people out, and those fixes help everyone else too.P1–P3, P6, C2–C4, K1–K4
Respect people’s timeEvery click, wait or scroll-jack is a tax on attention. Essentials show with zero actions; everything else is at most one away.P4, I1
Glance, then Scan, then ExplorePeople scan before they read, and most leave early (NN/g). Answer first, detail next, play last and opt-in (progressive disclosure).P5, PT1–PT9
Color carries mood, never meaning aloneFor red-green color blindness, green and orange collapse to the same brown unless they differ in lightness: this site’s own green and orange measured 1.0:1 under protanopia (learnings.md, 2026-10-19). WCAG makes it a rule (Use of Color).C3, C4, C5
Fast is a featureMost visitors are on a mid-range phone. Slow first paint loses them before the design is seen (web.dev).PF1–PF8
One wow per pageOne signature move is remembered; five compete and read as noise. Motion must never delay reading.P8, T8
Made for its subjectTemplated defaults read as “nobody decided this”. In Taste Skill’s 247-site study, the idea taken from the subject separated the best sites from the forgettable ones.T1, T2, T6, T9
